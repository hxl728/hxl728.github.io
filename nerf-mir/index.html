NeRF-MIR: Toward High-Quality Restoration of Masked Images With Neural Radiance Fields

IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, 2026

Xianliang Huang, Zhizhou Zhong, Shuhang Chen, Yi Xu, Jihong Guan, Shuigeng Zhou

DOI arXiv

Overview

NeRF-MIR adapts neural radiance fields to masked image restoration. It improves the native NeRF ray-emission strategy for image processing and restores masked regions by using multi-view information from corrupted captures.

Highlights

  • Patch-based entropy ray emitting strategy for texture-aware ray allocation.
  • Progressively iterative restoration for masked regions.
  • Dynamically weighted loss for masked and non-masked regions.
  • Masked datasets constructed from LLFF, Spaces, and Blender scenes.
