Xianliang Huang

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate at the School of Computer Science, Fudan University. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Shuigeng Zhou in the ADMIS Lab. Before joining Fudan, I received my M.S. degree in Radio Physics from Xiamen University and my B.S. degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Nanchang University.

My research interests include computer vision, computer graphics, neural rendering, image inpainting, and 3D reconstruction. I am especially interested in reconstructing 3D faces and scenes with neural radiance fields, differentiable rendering, diffusion priors, and Gaussian splatting, including restoration of contaminated or distracted scenes.

Research

I work on neural rendering and 3D vision, with a focus on multi-view face generation, physically guided 3D face and scene reconstruction, neural radiance fields, differentiable rendering, diffusion-based image inpainting, object removal, and Gaussian-splatting-based scene recovery.
