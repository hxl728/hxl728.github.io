DR-Net: A Multi-View Face Synthesis Network Driven by Dual Representation

IEEE International Conference on Multimedia and Expo (ICME) 2023, Oral, CCF-B

Xianliang Huang, Yining Lang, Ying Guo, Yuan He, Hui Xue, Li Zhao, Shuigeng Zhou

Paper PDF Code pending Video pending

Overview

DR-Net is a multi-view face synthesis framework driven by both geometry and learning representations. It combines 3D face geometry with a learning-based synthesis branch, then uses region attention guided by face parsing to dynamically mix the two representations across different face regions.

Highlights

  • Dual representation design combines geometry-based and learning-based synthesis.
  • Region attention network assigns adaptive weights to semantic face regions.
  • Geometry information improves identity preservation and in-the-wild robustness.
  • Modular framework can replace geometry or learning branches with other methods.
